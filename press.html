Rory — Points and Award Travel

A free wallet for your points that tells you what's worth doing — and by when.

What it is

You tell Rory what cards and points you hold — no bank linking, no passwords. The wallet is free. Rory Premium (14 days free, then $69 a year) adds the judgment: a weekly check of the offers and transfer bonuses that apply to your cards, with the dates; a welcome-bonus goal built from your own numbers; deferred-interest purchases carried against their own deadlines; and answers about your wallet that end in a verdict. It never recommends a card you don't hold. No ads, no card bounties.
