What Rory does

Four jobs, done in a banker's voice.

The Feed

Ranked by attention, not by what pays us.

Every live transfer bonus, elevated offer, and points sale, read and given a verdict — including the ones that say skip. Urgency comes first, then what's genuinely new. Most weeks the honest answer is to do nothing, and Rory says so.

The Wallet

One line for what needs a decision today.

Your balances sit under a single "for your review" line that surfaces only what deserves attention now. Goals live on the card that funds them, tracked quietly. No vanity totals, no urgency theater.

Rory

Answers with the one number that matters.

Rory knows the cards you hold and meets a new welcome offer with what it's worth to you, not the headline bonus. When it's worth chasing, he proposes the goal and tracks the spend. When it isn't, he tells you plainly.

Defense

The fire alarm no partner can buy.

Announced devaluations reach you early, while the old chart still books. Stealth changes reach you fast and personally, matched to the balances you actually hold. No program pays to soften that warning.
